SetuScan

Crack inspection that measures in millimetres, looks closer when unsure, and asks an engineer before raising a ticket.

Where things run. This page and its OpenCV 5 WebAssembly build are static files served from AWS (S3 behind CloudFront). The image analysis runs in your browser on your device; photos are not uploaded anywhere.

Decision support only. Not a structural certification. Severity bands are an example policy, not a design-code standard.
